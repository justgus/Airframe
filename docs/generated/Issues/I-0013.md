## I-0013: Epic close eligibility ignores open Sprints

**Status:** Implemented - Verified
**GitHub Issue:** #151
**Severity:** High
**Epic:** EP-018
**Sprint Assigned:** SP-023
**Date Reported:** TBD
**Date Resolved:** 2026-06-25
**Date Verified:** 2026-06-25

**Observed Behavior:**
AgileCockpit reports the active Epic as eligible to close even when related Sprints such as SP-023 and SP-024 are not completed yet.

**Expected Behavior:**
Epic close eligibility should require all related Sprints for the Epic to be closed, or otherwise explicitly excluded by workflow policy, before enabling Epic close.

**Reproduction Steps:**
1. Open AgileCockpit with EP-018 active.
2. Leave SP-023 Active and SP-024 Planning.
3. Observe that Epic close eligibility still reports eligible.

**Affected Components:**
- AgileCockpit
- AirframeCore
- Canonical State

**Evidence:**
- TBD

**Notes:**
- Acceptance Criteria: 1. Epic close eligibility reports blocked when any related Sprint is Backlog, Planning, Active, or Review. 2. The blocking message identifies the open Sprint IDs. 3. The Close Epic button remains disabled while related Sprints are open. 4. Regression coverage proves an Epic with open Sprints cannot be closed.
- Resolution: Epic close eligibility now includes related Sprint status and blocks close while related Sprints remain Backlog, Planning, Active, or Review.
