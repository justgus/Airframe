## I-0028: Add AICockpit management for Tasks, Sprints, and Epics

**Status:** Backlog
**GitHub Issue:** TBD

**Epic:** EP-024
**Sprint Assigned:** TBD

**Observed Behavior:**
AICockpit supports some work-item creation and status transitions, but it does not yet provide a complete management surface for Tasks, Sprints, and Epics with rich link management to acceptance criteria, requirements, and tests.

**Expected Behavior:**
AICockpit should provide first-class management for Tasks, Sprints, and Epics, including create, list, inspect, update, status transitions, and cross-linking to acceptance criteria, requirements, and tests without requiring direct canonical JSON edits.

**Reproduction Steps:**
1. Inspect AICockpit help for artifact-specific create and update options for tasks, sprints, and epics.
2. Observe that the available commands do not yet cover the full management surface for those work products.
3. Attempt to manage links from tasks, sprints, and epics to ACs, requirements, and tests entirely through AICockpit.
4. Observe that some relationships still require direct canonical JSON edits or are not first-class commands.

- AICockpit

**Notes:**
- This issue absorbs the broader artifact-management scope that was split out of I-0027.
- The target surface includes full lifecycle management for Tasks, Sprints, and Epics, not just their creation shells.
- The command surface should support links between work products and traceability artifacts so agents can manage the graph from the CLI instead of editing repo-local JSON.
- Acceptance Criteria: AICockpit can create, list, inspect, update, and transition Tasks, Sprints, and Epics through canonical commands.
- Acceptance Criteria: AICockpit can link Tasks, Sprints, and Epics to one another and to acceptance criteria, requirements, and tests.
- Acceptance Criteria: AICockpit can report coverage and missing-link gaps for the artifact graph.
- Acceptance Criteria: Markdown export renders the work-product relationships consistently from canonical state.
- Acceptance Criteria: Tests cover the new management commands and the traceability graph behavior.
