## I-0030: Epic criteria verification freezes detail view

**Status:** Implemented - Verified
**GitHub Issue:** TBD
**Severity:** High
**Epic:** EP-024
**Sprint Assigned:** TBD
**Date Reported:** 2026-07-07
**Date Resolved:** 2026-07-07
**Date Verified:** 2026-07-07

**Observed Behavior:**
On the Sprint & Epic Detail View, Epic Criteria tab, marking an acceptance criterion as verified takes many seconds, roughly 30 seconds per verification, and the app shows the spinning beachball during the operation.

**Expected Behavior:**
Marking an Epic acceptance criterion as verified should complete promptly without blocking the main UI thread or causing the app to beachball.

**Reproduction Steps:**
1. Open the Sprint & Epic Detail View.
2. Select an Epic and open the Epic Criteria tab.
3. Mark an acceptance criterion as verified.
4. Observe that the operation takes roughly 30 seconds and the app beachballs before completing.

**Affected Components:**
- AgileCockpit
- AirframeCore
- Epic Criteria
