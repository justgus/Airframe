**Rationale:**
A canonical mutation can succeed while its projections silently go stale, leaving generated documents wrong with no signal. This task gives mutation success and projection freshness an explicit consistency contract, and makes refresh failure visible rather than mistakable for success.

Constraint: Canonical mutation success and projection freshness must have an explicit consistency contract.

1. Successful canonical mutations trigger or clearly schedule complete projection refresh.
2. Human verification refresh is covered by regression tests.
3. Refresh failure is surfaced and cannot be mistaken for success.
